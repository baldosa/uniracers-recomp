Uniracers Web

Runs in your browser on the snesrecomp interpreter. No game is included: pick your own Uniracers (USA) ROM. It stays on your machine; saves and settings are kept in this browser. Play alone or two players on one keyboard, or online: one player creates a game and shares the code.
